This project highlights my research-oriented experimental work on **safety**, **security**, and **resilience** using the **Quanser** ecosystem.

The work combines physical and simulated platforms to design and study experiments involving:

- **QCar**
- **QDrone**
- traffic-light and roadway interaction modules
- perception, state estimation, and control activities
- resilience-oriented testing under faults or adversarial conditions

The platform use and experimentation are carried out in collaboration with [Dr. Hossein Rastgoftar](https://ame.engineering.arizona.edu/faculty-staff/faculty/hossein-rastgoftar).
